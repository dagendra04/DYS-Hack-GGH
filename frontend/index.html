Real-Time Voice Translation • Hindi • English

Speak your language.
Be heard in theirs.

Connect on a natural two-person browser voice call. Each side's speech is automatically transcribed, translated, and spoken aloud in the other person's preferred language. Complete with instant AI call summaries.

Supported Languages:

1. Speech to Text

Local microphone audio is transcribed into text using browser speech recognition or server AI (Gemini / OpenAI).

2. Neural Translation

Sentences are translated between Hindi and English seamlessly, preserving context and meaning.

3. Voice Synthesis

The translated message is spoken aloud to the participant with natural text-to-speech audio.

4. AI Call Summary

Get structured summaries, key points, decisions, and action items instantly after or during your call.

Privacy & Data Notice

This app requires access to your microphone. Speech and text are processed for transcription, translation, and speech synthesis. The server does not store audio, transcripts, or call records. Calls use standard WebRTC transport encryption. Your call transcript remains private in your browser session.
