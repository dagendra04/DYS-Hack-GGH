Speak your language.
Be heard in theirs.

A two-person browser voice call. Each side's speech is transcribed, translated and spoken aloud in the other person's language. Expect a few seconds of delay — this is a sequential speech → text → translation → speech pipeline, not instant interpretation.

Privacy notice

This app uses your microphone. Depending on server settings, your speech and its text are sent to third-party AI services (e.g. Google Gemini, OpenAI) for transcription, translation and speech. In Chrome, browser speech recognition also sends audio to Google. The server does not store audio or transcripts. Calls are not guaranteed private beyond standard WebRTC transport encryption; translated text passes through this server.
